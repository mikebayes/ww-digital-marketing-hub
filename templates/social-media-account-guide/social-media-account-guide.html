01 Platforms & Cadence

Facebook 3 posts per week. Primary channel.
Instagram 3 posts per week, mirrored from Facebook with vertical crops. Stories ad hoc.
LinkedIn 2 posts per month. Instructor recruitment and community news only.
Planned Monthly calendar, submitted for approval in the last week of the prior month.

02 Audience & Priorities

  • Primary: parents of children aged 3–12 in Oakville and west Burlington.
  • Secondary: adults in learn-to-swim and stroke-improvement classes — an underfilled program the client wants grown.
  • Registration windows drive the year. Fall and winter intakes matter most; adult programs are the growth priority for 2027.
  • Instructor recruitment runs continuously on LinkedIn.

03 Content Direction

Pillars Lesson moments · Water-safety tips · Instructor and staff profiles · Registration and program news
Messages Small class sizes, instructors who stay with the same group, warm pool.
Avoid Fear-based drowning statistics, competitor comparisons, anything implying guaranteed outcomes.
Voice Warm and plain. Short sentences, first person plural, no exclamation stacking. Written for a tired parent on a phone.

04 Content Sources

Client Pool and lesson photography, roughly quarterly. Program dates and pricing.
Web Wizards All copy, all graphic design, story templates.
Staff Instructor profiles — Maya collects short answers from each instructor before the term starts.
Library Shared Drive folder, linked below. Consent-cleared images are in the Approved subfolder only.

05 Approval & Community

Approver Maya Okonjo, Program Director. Sole approver.
Method Full monthly calendar by email. Comments in the shared doc.
Turnaround Three business days. Chase on day four.
Community Not included. The client answers comments and DMs. We do not reply on their behalf.
Escalate Anything about a child’s safety or a complaint goes to Maya the same day, by phone.

06 Dates & Constraints

  • Registration opens mid-Aug (fall), early Dec (winter) and late Apr (spring). Content ramps two weeks before each.
  • March Break camp promoted from late Jan.
  • Summer camp promoted from late Apr.
  • No child appears in content without written consent. Use the Approved subfolder; if in doubt, do not post it.
  • No safety guarantees or outcome claims — regulated language for the client’s insurer.
